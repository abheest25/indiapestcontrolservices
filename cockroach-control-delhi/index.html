Our Cockroach Treatment Process

1. Initial Inspection: Technicians identify the cockroach species, assess infestation level and locate hotspots like kitchens, bathrooms and appliances.

2. Gel Bait Application: Specialized low-toxicity gel is applied in cabinets, corners and electrical areas. Cockroaches consume it and spread it to others, killing the entire colony.

3. Crack & Crevice Treatment: Insecticidal dust is applied inside wall voids and hidden areas for long-term control.

4. Targeted Spraying: Liquid insecticide is applied along baseboards and behind appliances to kill insects on contact.

5. Insect Growth Regulators (IGRs): Stops breeding by preventing eggs from hatching and nymphs from maturing.

6. Monitoring & Traps: Sticky traps are placed to track activity and evaluate treatment effectiveness.

7. Exclusion & Hygiene Advice: Guidance on sealing cracks and maintaining hygiene to prevent reinfestation.

8. Follow-up Visit: Second treatment after 2–3 weeks ensures complete elimination.
