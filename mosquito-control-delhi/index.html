Our Mosquito Treatment Process

1. Site Inspection & Assessment:

  • Identify breeding sites like standing water (gutters, buckets, tanks)
  • Locate resting areas (vegetation, shaded spaces)
  • Determine mosquito species (Aedes, Culex, Anopheles)

2. Source Reduction & Habitat Modification:

  • Drain stagnant water
  • Remove waste containers
  • Trim overgrown vegetation

3. Larviciding (Preventative Treatment):

  • Apply larvicides in water bodies
  • Use eco-friendly and safe agents
  • Prevent larvae from developing into adults

4. Adulticiding (Fogging & Spraying):

  • Thermal Fogging for dense areas
  • Cold Fogging (ULV) for airborne control
  • Residual spray for long-term protection

5. Post-Treatment & Monitoring:

  • Safe re-entry after 30–60 minutes
  • Regular monitoring and AMC visits
