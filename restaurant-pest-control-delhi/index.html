Pest Problems in Restaurants

Restaurants are highly vulnerable to pests due to food storage, waste and moisture. Cockroaches, flies and rodents can lead to hygiene issues, customer complaints and even closure by authorities. Our expert pest control ensures your business stays compliant and safe.

Restaurant Pest Control FAQ

How often does a restaurant need pest control?
Most restaurants require service every 30–60 days. High-risk kitchens benefit from monthly visits.

Are chemicals safe for food areas?
Yes, we use food-safe, HACCP-compliant treatments applied carefully.

Do you provide documentation for inspections?
Yes, we provide complete logs and reports for FSSAI audits.

Why isn’t cleaning enough?
Cleaning helps, but pests enter through gaps, deliveries and drains. Professional control is essential.
